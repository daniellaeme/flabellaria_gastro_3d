**Isolated \& Quantified Compounds from *Daniellia oliveri* and <i>Flabellaria paniculata</i>**

Source: Published pharmacognosy and isolation works of Prof. M. O. Sofidiya.



1\. ***Flabellaria paniculata Cav. (Malpighiaceae)***

Source Literature: Gastroprotective Effect and Chemical Constituents of Flabellaria paniculata

(Malpighiaceae) (Sofidiya et al.) 



Extraction/Isolation Method: Column chromatography of the

Ethyl Acetate (EtOAc) fraction of methanolic leaf extract, elucidated via NMR and Mass

Spectrometry.



Isolated Triterpenoids:

Friedelin

Friedelinol



Isolated Steroids:

Sitosterol (β-Sitosterol)

Sitosterol-β-D-glucoside



Isolated Flavonoid Glycosides:

Kaempferol-3-O-α-L-rhamnopyranosyl-(1→6)-β-D-glucopyranoside (Rutin / Nicotiflorin

variant)



2\. ***Daniellia oliveri (Rolfe) Hutch. \& Dalziel (Fabaceae)***

Source Literature: Anti-inflammatory and antinociceptive activities of Daniellia oliveri (Fabaceace)

stem bark extract (Sofidiya et al., 2023, Journal of Ethnopharmacology) 



Extraction/Identification Method: Methanolic stem bark extract, identified and quantified via HPLC-DAD-UV.



Phenolic Acids:

Caffeic acid

p-Coumaric acid

Ferulic acid



Flavonoids \& Flavonoid Glycosides:

Rutin

Apigenin-7-glucoside

Quercetin

Kaempferol
